The pivot appears here after you run the lookups
  1. Exception report: AWB/MANIFEST NO, INBOUND DATE, MOVED TO GA and EXP STATUS are looked up against Awb_number (first match, files in zip order). AWBs not in the reports but on the orphan list take the orphan's MOVED TO GA.

Lookups follow Excel VLOOKUP rules: exact match on a trimmed, case-insensitive AWB, first hit wins, a matched blank returns 0 and no match returns #N/A.
